01 — About

Working the seam between healthcare AI and the security it has to survive in.

I split my time between a classroom, a server room, and a manuscript queue. At Pacific States University — part of the Konkuk University Foundation — I teach STEM courses and run the IT and cybersecurity infrastructure, which keeps my research grounded in systems that have to actually stay up.

My research looks at where AI meets fragile, high-stakes data: single-lead ECG signals from wearables, diabetic foot ulcer imaging, sepsis prediction from clinical time series, and the federated and adversarial conditions that determine whether any of it survives contact with a real hospital network. The same instinct shows up in my cybersecurity work — malware analysis tooling, threat detection pipelines, blockchain-based defense — built by someone who also has to patch the systems the students use.
